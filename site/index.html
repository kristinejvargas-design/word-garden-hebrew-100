WHY START HERE?

Frequency is a trailhead,
not the whole forest.

Logos’ new guide to Hebrew vocabulary describes frequency lists, word roots, semantic groupings, and learning words in Scripture context. This deck keeps one small frequency-first step, then anchors each word in a verse.

You may have heard about the “50× words.” That means words occurring at least fifty times—not a list of fifty words. One vocabulary-in-context resource covers 642 such words and reports roughly 90% coverage of the Old Testament. First 100 is a friendly beginning, not a shortcut around grammar or reading.

GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
before you begin.

How should I learn biblical Hebrew vocabulary?

Use active recall: look at the pointed Hebrew, try to remember the meaning, then reveal it. Short, regular sessions and spaced review help words stick. Seeing a word in a verse also gives the gloss a place to live.

What are the “50× words”?

It’s a frequency threshold: words appearing at least 50 times in the Hebrew Bible. Logos’ guide describes a vocabulary-in-context resource with 642 non-proper-name words at that threshold and reports about 90% coverage. This 100-word starter set is not the same list or coverage claim.

Why are there so many short words near the top?

High-frequency lists include particles, pronouns, and conjunctions. Some Hebrew words have no single English equivalent, and Hebrew can attach short prefixes to a word. We group those forms with their base Strong’s lemma so the study set stays useful for reading.

How does the review schedule work?

After revealing a card, choose Again, Hard, Good, or Easy. The next review is scheduled a little later each time. Your schedule is saved in this browser on this device; it is not sent to an account.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The trainer runs in your browser and is free to use. A stable internet connection is needed the first time to load the vocabulary file; review progress stays local in your browser storage.
